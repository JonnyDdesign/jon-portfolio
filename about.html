About Me

After working as a special education teacher for five years and commercial real estate appraiser for nearly six years, I’ve decided to take the leap and pursue a career in web development.

My journey in web development began on Codecademy, and my knowledge continues to expand every day. My portfolio site was designed with an Arizona theme. The color scheme represents a serene Arizona desert sunset, and also represents the color of my favorite basketball team, the Phoenix Suns! Additionally, my logo was inspired by the Suns logo. This site was entirely hand-coded via HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript.

I love to learn new technologies and solve problems to create amazing projects. I'm open to career opportunities where I can contribute, learn, and grow. If you have an opportunity that matches my skills and experience, please reach out and contact me.
